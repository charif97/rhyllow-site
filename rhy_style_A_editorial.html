I create art through movement.

RHY — Guadalupe Sánchez — is a Madrid-based dancer from the Vallekas neighborhood, working across contemporary, commercial and floorwork. She builds an international career between Madrid's nightlife stages and Morocco's luxury dinner-show circuit, cast through international agencies for festivals, residencies, dance films and music videos.

Her style blends high-level floorwork, acrobatic conditioning and commercial pop choreography — physical, precise and emotionally raw. Alongside performing, she teaches open classes in Madrid.

“Find what you love and let it kill you.”

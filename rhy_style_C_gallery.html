02 — About

A dancer who turns emotion into gesture.

Madrid-born and Vallekas-raised, RHY specializes in high-octane commercial choreography, elite nightlife entertainment and intense contemporary floorwork. She fuses athletic conditioning and acrobatic technique with atmospheric storytelling on camera and stage.

Cast through international agencies, she works across residencies, festivals, dance films and music videos between Spain and Morocco, and teaches open classes in Madrid.

About

Movement with intention. Presence that carries a room.

Guadalupe Sánchez — known on stage as RHY — is a professional dancer whose work spans the luxury hospitality circuit, live events, music video and festival stages. Her range moves fluidly between commercial energy, contemporary depth, grounded floorwork and heels.

From residencies at Palais Dar Soukkar in Marrakech to the stages of Madrid, she brings a cinematic physicality and a fearless approach to performance — «miro al miedo con coraje».

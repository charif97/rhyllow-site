Guadalupe Sánchez — RHY — is a professional dancer moving between the luxury performance circuit of Morocco and the stages of Madrid. From dinner-show residencies in Marrakech and Rabat to music video, festival and live-club work, she brings cinematic physicality and a fearless presence across commercial, contemporary, floorwork and heels.

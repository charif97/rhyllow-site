About

RHY — Guadalupe Sánchez — is a Madrid-born dancer from Vallekas, specializing in high-octane commercial choreography, elite nightlife entertainment and intense contemporary floorwork.

Known for fusing athletic conditioning and acrobatic technique with atmospheric storytelling on camera and stage, she is cast through international agencies for residencies, festivals, dance films and music videos across Spain and Morocco.
